## Selection and transform gates

- Candidates require an exact six-field offer match, source URL/title/author, at least two ingredients, at least three contiguous numeric steps, a quantified primary ingredient, and at least 100 reviews.
- Selection uses existing Bayesian quality ranking, then prioritizes low-count locations and identity/family/method/author diversity. The deterministic 120-item shortlist targets 96 mains, 12 breakfasts, and 12 sides before editorial holds.
- New entries are keyed by `sourceRecipeId|sourceContentHash|transformVersion`, set `approved:true`, and record `approvalMethod:"owner-authorized-editorial-transform"`, source date 2026-09-07, and approval date 2026-09-11. No named human reviewer is claimed.
- Validation rejects copied source sentences or six-token runs, HTML/unknown raw-source fields, invented or lost numeric/heat/preparation facts, short action summaries, non-Korean titles/steps, unsafe raw-animal use anywhere in the recipe, and primary ingredients outside canonical offer evidence. Source action ingredients without a recorded quantity are included as `(원문 수량 미표기)` and become quantity-check/partial-cost shopping items rather than fabricated quantities.
- Public snapshot output contains only compiler-approved detail fields. Raw steps, images, HTML, the CSV, and the private candidate queue remain outside the public tree.
- Action-ingredient closure treats alternative cooking oils as one choice. It recognizes generic `오일`, sunflower and coconut oil, prefers the oil explicitly listed by the source, ignores topic-particle negations such as `기름은 넣지 않아도`, `대신 … 써도`, conditional alternatives, rendered pan fat, and other negative phrases, and records an unmeasured required oil once with `(원문 수량 미표기)`. Bare sesame oil remains sesame oil and does not become a generic oil purchase; sesame oil also does not satisfy a separate generic pan-oil action. A full scan of 17 published fat-action records found zero missing or extraneous purchase rows. Shopping strips qualitative quantity suffixes, merges branded and generic cooking-oil aliases plus spaced melted butter, resolves and retains a specific offer before collapsing its grocery key, and migrates pantry, prices, quantities, and saved rows. Grocery rendering prefers the persisted chosen product and evidence over a catalog fallback. A snapshot rollover resets saved quantities and quantity-certainty flags before reuse.
- Candidate matches carry and revalidate the complete six-field offer identity, the scoped offer ID, canonical ingredient aliases, and an actual source ingredient label before publication. Raw offers reject processed, minced, or wrong-cut animal forms. Raw checks cover eggs, mixed meat, flatfish, fish, seafood, duck, and lamb while recognizing explicitly prepared shrimp/chicken/roast forms and excluding derived seasonings such as stock, fish sauce, and salmon sauce.
- Each location recipe reference carries its approved editorial title inside the hash-pinned snapshot. The validator requires that title to equal the hashed detail title, and the runtime uses it for the first menu, recommendation, and plan render. All 11 locations have one distinct visible title per recipe, including 28 of 28 at 52064 EDEKA.
- Source links require the exact HTTPS URL `https://www.10000recipe.com/recipe/<sourceRecipeId>` in selection, snapshot validation, and release verification. The browser repeats the same check and removes the link when the URL is absent or unsafe.
- Detail shopping rows label fallback package prices as `확인된 1팩 가격` and show `수량 확인`; their section label describes a checked-price subtotal rather than a definitive total.

## Remaining truthful limits

Three canonical offer identities and five offer rows still have zero recipe candidates in the source corpus. Complete basket pricing and measured nutrient facts are still absent, so value, nutrition, and diet modes remain unavailable rather than displaying invented scores. When a basket has structured gram or milliliter requirements, shopping rows persist them by recipe ID: re-adding the same recipe is idempotent, while different recipes aggregate requirements before package rounding. The public detail boundary still carries display strings rather than a complete unit-normalized amount model, so prose and qualitative amounts remain partial with `수량 확인` instead of producing false exact totals. The weekly `장보기 준비` path aggregates all planned meals before package calculation. Browser CUA remains the separate release gate owned by Task 7/final release validation.
